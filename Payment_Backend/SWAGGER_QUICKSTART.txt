┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  📚 AVAILABLE ENDPOINTS                                       │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

    📦 PRODUCTS (4 endpoints)
    ├─ GET  /api/products                    All products
    ├─ GET  /api/products/subscriptions      Subscriptions
    ├─ GET  /api/products/one-time           One-time items
    └─ GET  /api/products/{id}               Specific product

    💳 PURCHASES (4 endpoints)
    ├─ POST /api/purchases/validate          Validate receipt
    ├─ GET  /api/purchases/history/{userId}  Purchase history
    ├─ GET  /api/purchases/{purchaseId}      Get purchase
    └─ POST /api/purchases/{id}/acknowledge  Acknowledge

    📅 SUBSCRIPTIONS (3 endpoints)
    ├─ GET  /api/subscriptions/active/{id}   Active sub
    ├─ GET  /api/subscriptions/history/{id}  Sub history
    └─ POST /api/subscriptions/{id}/cancel   Cancel sub
